How to Use Everyday Calculators

Our free online calculators are designed to make everyday tasks easier. Here are a few simple ways to use them.

How to Calculate a Tip

Enter your restaurant bill amount and choose a tip percentage. The Tip Calculator shows the tip amount and the total bill, helping you plan your payment.

How to Calculate a Discount

Enter the original price of an item and the discount percentage. The Discount Calculator helps you find how much you save and the final sale price.

How to Split a Bill

Enter the total bill and the number of people sharing it. The Split the Bill calculator divides the amount equally so everyone can see how much to pay.

How to Calculate a Percentage

Enter the part and the whole to find what percentage one number represents of another. This can help with everyday math and comparisons.

How to Convert Length

Choose a starting unit and a target unit, enter a value, and convert measurements between feet, inches, and meters.

How to Count Words and Characters

Paste or type text into the Word and Character Counter to quickly see the number of words and characters in your text.

How to Calculate Your Age

Enter your date of birth to calculate your age in years, months, and days.
